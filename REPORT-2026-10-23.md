# Tuning study, 2026-10-23

This report replaces an earlier version of this file. That version, and the README of commits
`ed655c3` to `444a42a`, stated 840 to 898 tok/s at eight streams and an eight-slot deployment. The
figure was the single-stream rate times eight, not a measurement, and eight slots were never
deployed. Every number below was measured.

## Setup

The baseline is commit `ed0cb7e`, which was production revision `SERVICE-qhead-h2`: 2 slots of
block 8, fp32 recurrent state, `--mem-fraction-static 0.80`, `containerConcurrency` 4, context
262,144. Each configuration ran on its own test service in the production region on ½ RTX PRO 6000, with the
same image digest, the same AWQ weights and the same DFlash 2 drafter. The
client was one machine over the internet. Production was not used for the study.

- `bench2` is `scripts/bench2.py`: seven workloads of two prompts each, greedy, thinking off, up to
  800 tokens, one stream. A rate is (tokens - 1) / (last token - first token); the headline is the
  unweighted mean of the seven workload means.
- `bench3` is `scripts/bench3.py URL out.json 1 2 4 8`: the same fourteen prompts at 1, 2, 4 and 8
  concurrent streams. The total is every completion token over the wall time of the round, so
  prefill and queueing count.
- Token-weighted is sum(tokens - 1) / sum(decode time) over the one-stream round of `bench3`.
- Each configuration got one warm-up round, then five `bench2` and five `bench3` runs; the
  eight-slot configuration at 0.79 and 2 × 16 with torch-compile got three of each. Medians, with
  all runs in brackets.

## Quality

- 4 × 8 fp32 returns byte-identical greedy text to 2 × 8 on 104 of 104 prompts of 256 tokens. On
  `scripts/qcon.py` it answers 64 of 64 alone and at four streams, with no words from the other
  requests. Four photos are recognized alone and eight of eight at four concurrent requests.
- 8 × 8 with fp32 state (pool 203,338 tokens at 0.80, too small for the 262,144 context) also gives
  104 of 104. The slot count, the batch-size-8 CUDA graphs and the retiled draft head are therefore
  bit-neutral at one stream.
- 8 × 8 with bf16 state gives 41 of 104 byte-identical answers, and 8 of 24 on `scripts/drift.py`.
  Teacher-forced over 191,301 positions of 46 documents (`scripts/fid.py`), its KL to fp32 state is
  0.0015 (Chinese 0.0056, code 0.0000), top-1 agreement 99.69%, perplexity 3.722 for both. Every
  task check is equal to 2 × 8: needle 5/5 at 30k and 120k, math 12/12, refusals 0/6, `qcon.py`
  64/64 at eight streams, GSM8K 193 of the first 200 test problems for both (192 right in both),
  no loops in eight greedy and 24 sampled 4,000-token answers, vision 16/16 at eight concurrent
  requests, `apitest.py` unchanged. A 261,074-token prompt is served and a 269,179-token prompt is
  refused with 400, as on 2 × 8.

## Memory

At `--mem-fraction-static 0.80`, 8 × 8 bf16 left 4.67 GB after startup and 4 × 8 fp32 5.00 GB. On
both, one `/generate` with full-prompt top-20 input logprobs (`scripts/fid.py`) asked for 1.89 GiB,
hit CUDA OOM and killed the server. 2 × 8 at 0.80 (5.21 GB left) survived the same request, and so
did 8 × 8 bf16 at 0.79 (5.17 GB left). Production therefore runs 0.79.

## Deployed

On 2026-10-23 production moved to revision `SERVICE-s4b8-fp32`: 4 × 8, fp32 state, mem 0.79,
`--max-mamba-cache-size 22`, `--cuda-graph-max-bs-decode 4`, `SGLANG_DFLASH_DRAFT_HEAD_CFG=128,128,8,2`,
`containerConcurrency` 8, `google-cloud-storage==3.17.1`. It reports a 266,083-token pool,
`max_req_input_len` 262,138 and 5.49 GiB left after startup. Checks on the production revision:

- `bench2` 140.5 tok/s (prose 89.2, chat 98.1, translation 99.3, summary 118.0, code 193.4,
  math 180.8, JSON 204.6); prose with thinking on and sampling 80.7.
- Four concurrent 800-token prose essays, thinking off: 230.1 and 232.1 tok/s in total (13.8 to
  13.9 s), against 133.3 and 143.0 (22 to 24 s) on the two-slot revision.
- The same image answer on both revisions for 4 of 4 requests (the image reads "ZEBRA 7341"; both
  answer "ZEBRA 741"), parallel tool calls work, and the server reports 4 running requests,
  float32 state and context 262,144.

8 × 8 bf16 at 0.79 stays an option: it reaches 584.6 tok/s at eight streams but changes greedy
text, and the clients of this deployment send at most four requests at once.

## Not adopted

- 2 × 16: `bench2` +9% from its workload mix only. Code, math and JSON are 15 to 30% faster, prose,
  chat and summary 5 to 7% slower, and token-weighted decode is equal. No multi-slot throughput.
- torch-compile: nothing at two slots (153.1 against 153.4); at eight slots CUDA-graph capture took
  about six minutes per batch size, beyond the 24-minute startup probe.
- Configurations above 32 draft rows per verify batch failed at startup with Triton
  `OutOfResources` (131,072 bytes of shared memory needed, 101,376 available) in the int8
  draft-head kernel, until `SGLANG_DFLASH_DRAFT_HEAD_CFG=128,128,8,2` (98,304 bytes).
- Two-batch overlap and an int4 draft head crash with DFLASH; the DP path selector lowers acceptance
  from 4.06 to 2.48 tokens per verification; 24 or 32 slots of block 2; fine-tuned drafters of
  block 8 and 16; the BF16 drafter.
